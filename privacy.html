Privacy notice

EPROM Strategic Tanks — internal status sheet. Last updated 3 October 2026.

This site is a work tool for EPROM staff to view and update the status of strategic crude-oil storage tanks. It is not open to the public: only people whose Google account is on the staff list can see the data.

What we receive when you sign in

You sign in with your Google account. Google tells the site your e-mail address and name. We use them only to check that you are on the staff list and to show your name on the screen.

What we keep

  • The tank readings and notes you enter, with your e-mail and the time, so changes can be traced.
  • The staff list: e-mail, name and role.

This is kept in a private Google Sheet owned by the site's administrator. A sign-in token is kept in your own browser so you stay signed in; signing out or clearing the browser removes it.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your information with anyone.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We do not read your Gmail, Drive or any other Google data.

Questions or removal

To ask what is held about you, or to be removed from the staff list, contact the site administrator through your EPROM line manager.
